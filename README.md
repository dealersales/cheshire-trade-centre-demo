# Cheshire Trade Centre — demo preview

Static preview of a dealer website built for Cheshire Trade Centre (Chester) on the
Digital Sales Consultants dealer template, incorporating a rebuild of the finance
landing page the client currently uses for paid traffic.

- Preview: https://dealersales.github.io/cheshire-trade-centre-demo/
- This is a **preview, not the live shopfront**. Every page is noindexed.
- Stock is a sample of the client's own cars (18), captured from their live listings
  on 19 November 2026. Prices, mileage and specs are theirs.
- Static export: stock filtering, pagination beyond the exported pages, and the
  enquiry form do not work here. They work in the PHP build.
- Prepared by Digital Sales Consultants Ltd. Not to be pointed at a client domain.
